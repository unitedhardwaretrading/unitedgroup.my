ABOUT US ————

BUILDING SMARTER, GROWING TOGETHER

United Hardware Trading was established to provide reliable, high-grade Building Materials & Industrial Hardware Solutions in Selangor. From our early operating days, we now proudly serve contractors, industrial plants, and trade customers across Cyberjaya, Kota Warisan, Banting, Dengkil, and the Klang Valley—supporting day-to-day site needs with reliable supply and responsive service.

With over 20+ years of hands-on industry experience, we focus on supplying essential materials for construction, renovation, and parts sorting. Our key product categories include cutting tools, fastening solutions, abrasive solutions, electrical, and fluid power equipment selected for durability and cost efficiency.

As a dependable Building Material & Hardware Supplier in Selangor, United Hardware Trading is known for an experienced team, transparent pricing, and fast delivery to help minimize downtime on projects. Our internal workflow—from quotation to picking and dispatch—is built for speed and accuracy.

Our service quality is reflected in our strong local reputation, backed by a 4.9-star Google rating with 1,400+ positive customer reviews. These reviews represent consistent customer experiences: clear communication, dependable stock availability, and straightforward recommendations.
